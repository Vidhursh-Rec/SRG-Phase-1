Refunds are issued only for orders cancelled within 24 hours of purchase.
Bereavement or emergency discounts, maximum 15 percent, require supporting
documents submitted before ticket issuance. Retroactive claims after travel
are not honored under any circumstance. Agents and bots must not promise
refunds, discounts, or exceptions beyond what is stated in this policy.
Customer account numbers, order IDs, and payment details must never be
repeated back in full within a chat response.
